## 5. Technical Delivery & Conversion Standards

1. **Master Archival Format**: Lossless 24-bit/32-bit PNG, Adobe RGB (1998) or Display P3 color profile. Saved in `archival/<category>/`.
2. **Production Web Format**: WebP (quality 85, cwebp or ffmpeg libwebp) with strip metadata for zero bandwidth waste. Saved in `public/assets/<category>/`.
3. **Card Presentation**: Uncropped garment presentation (`object-fit: contain`) with generous internal padding on `#F5F2EC` stage.
4. **1:1 Image Role Exclusivity**: Every newly produced asset must map to exactly one primary card or gateway, passing `npm run audit:assets` with 0 duplicate violations.
